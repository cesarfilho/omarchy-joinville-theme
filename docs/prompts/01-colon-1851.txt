Title: Colon, 1851
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset: final desktop wallpaper for the Omarchy Joinville theme

SCENE
March 1851: the sailing barque Colon arriving through the Babitonga bay with Swiss, German and Norwegian settlers; mangrove shores, small wooded islands, the green wall of the Atlantic Forest and the Serra do Mar beyond.

COMPOSITION AND LIGHT
Early dawn, pearl mist lying on still water, first gold light on the sails, deep blue hills fading into fog; long reflections on the bay.

SHARED STYLE
Fine painterly pixel art in the spirit of nineteenth-century romantic landscape painting. Deliberate small pixel clusters, crisp silhouettes, articulated architecture and vegetation, coherent subtle texture. Palette anchored on the Joinville theme: deep night blue #0F1A2A, heraldic blue #1F4E8C, plaster white #EEEAE0, heraldic gold #E0B043, roof-tile red #E46F57, enxaimel timber brown #C28C60, palm green #62A86D, Babitonga water cyan #4FB8B4, orchid magenta #C876BB, molten-iron orange #E8833A. Humid subtropical atmosphere: mist on the Serra do Mar, wet surfaces, soft rain where noted. Romantic atmospheric depth with readable foreground detail and calm areas suitable for a desktop (keep the top-left and centre free of busy detail). Preserve each scene's time of day; do not impose golden sunset on nocturnal scenes. People are incidental and integrated into the same texture, never photorealistic or large outlined game sprites.

GLOBAL CONSTRAINTS
Avoid blur, smeared detail, coarse mosaic, random grain, excessive sharpening, text, logos, coats of arms, sports badges, signatures, borders and UI. No identifiable real people.

OUTPUT
Edge-to-edge 16:9, 3840x2160 PNG. No post-generation upscaling.

SCENE CONSTRAINTS
Single historical sailing ship, modest scale. No modern boats, ports or buildings.
